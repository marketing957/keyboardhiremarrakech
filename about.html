About Keyboard Hire Marrakech

Morocco's premier keyboard rental service with over 15 years of excellence

Our Story

Founded in 2009, Keyboard Hire Marrakech began as a small passion project by a group of musicians who understood the critical importance of having reliable, high-quality keyboard equipment for performances. What started with just 5 keyboards has grown into Morocco's leading keyboard rental service.

Over the past 15+ years, we've served over 500 clients, from international recording artists to local venues, educational institutions, and event organizers. Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction has made us the trusted name in keyboard rental throughout Morocco.

Today, we operate from our modern warehouse in Gueliz, Marrakech, with an extensive inventory of premium keyboards, stage pianos, synthesizers, and digital pianos from the world's leading brands.

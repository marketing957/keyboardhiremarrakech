About Keyboard Hire Marrakech

Since 2009, Keyboard Hire Marrakech has been Morocco's premier provider of professional keyboard rental services. Founded by passionate musicians and audio engineers, we understand the unique demands of live performances and studio recordings in Morocco's vibrant music scene.

Our mission is simple: to provide musicians, event organizers, and recording studios with access to world-class keyboard equipment without the burden of ownership costs. From intimate jazz performances in Marrakech's historic venues to large-scale music festivals across Morocco, our keyboards have been part of countless memorable musical moments.

We pride ourselves on our technical expertise, reliable service, and deep understanding of both traditional Moroccan music and contemporary genres. Our team consists of experienced musicians and sound engineers who can help you choose the perfect keyboard setup for your specific needs, whether you're performing classical Andalusian music, modern pop, or experimental electronic compositions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about keyboard rental in Marrakech

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for events, especially during peak season (May-September). However, we do offer same-day rental for urgent needs subject to availability.

Do you provide delivery and setup?

Yes, we provide professional delivery, setup, and pickup services throughout Marrakech. Our technical team ensures everything is perfectly configured for your event.

What if the equipment fails during my event?

We offer 24/7 emergency support and typically have backup equipment available. Our team can be on-site within 2 hours for any technical issues.

Are there any additional fees?

No hidden fees. Our pricing includes delivery, setup, and basic technical support within Marrakech. Additional charges may apply for services outside the city.

What brands do you carry?

We carry premium brands including Nord, Yamaha, Korg, Roland, Kawai, and Casio. All equipment is professionally maintained and regularly updated.

Do you offer long-term rental discounts?

Yes, we offer significant discounts for weekly and monthly rentals. Contact us for custom pricing on long-term projects and venue contracts.
